# 🚀 Episode 1 of Steph's Coding Adventures with Claude

## 🧩 Maze Runner

**Maze Runner** is a small reinforcement learning game where a robot agent learns to navigate a maze built by the player using **Q-learning**.

### 🎮 How to Play

1. **Build your maze** — Click and drag on the canvas to draw walls.
2. **Train the robot** — Click **Train** to let the robot learn how to navigate the maze.
3. **Watch it walk** — Click **Watch it Walk** to see the robot put its training to the test.
4. **Rate its intelligence** — How smart is your little robot? 🤖

### 🎵 Music

The background music starts when you first click on the screen. You can pause or resume it using the music button.

Have fun building mazes and watching your robot learn! 🧠🤖
